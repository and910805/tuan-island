# 茶葉展示櫃素材

用內建 ImageGen 產出透明底雙視角素材，匯入橫向 62×43 和縱向 30×69。櫃面預留茶葉展示位置，內容由遊戲依庫存繪製。

提示詞：Two isolated pixel art game sprites of the same low Taiwanese tea display cabinet in left and right halves of a genuine transparent canvas. Cozy 32px tile Taiwan farming game. Light honey bamboo cupboards, low wood plinth, open unobstructed flat counter surface on top for three tea tins added later by the game, paneled front with muted green blue smoky glass framed in bamboo, subtle bamboo nodes, forest-brown outer clusters. Left sprite horizontal wide two-tile axis front at orthographic three-quarter top down, silhouette planned for 62x43 pixels. Right sprite rotated in plane 90 degrees, the storage extends away from the camera with long narrow flat top, planned for 30x69 pixels. No products atop either, no letters, no backboard, no shelves rising above the flat counter, no other objects or shadows, a large gap between views. Crisp low resolution pixel clusters and matching palette.
