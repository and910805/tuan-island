# 公用耕田機架

《糰糰島》原創地圖物件。使用 Codex 內建圖像生成工具製作，依遊戲的暖木、森林綠與銅色配置繪製。

正式透明圖為 40×48 像素。物件靠下對齊，地面接觸點為從左上角算起的 (20, 46)。原圖與裁切、縮放流程存放於 `work/public-tiller-art`，透明邊緣保留清楚的像素輪廓。

生成提示如下。

Create an original isolated transparent-background pixel-art game prop for a Taiwanese cozy island farming game, a communal farm equipment loan rack beside a small farm. One compact narrow structure for a single 32-pixel map tile, displayed at an effective final 40 pixels wide by 48 pixels tall. Crisp 16-bit in-game pixel sprite, strong simple silhouettes, painted on a visible consistent square grid, fully readable small. Near top-down 3/4 tile game camera looking from south, straight horizontal roof front and vertical gravity; absolutely no diamond isometric view. A low pair of amber cedar uprights holds a tiny overhanging forest green shade roof; a clearly legible small hand-pushed tiller is resting on a narrow low plank deck under it. The tiller has a dark steel wheel on the left, a warm copper/terracotta chassis, and one diagonal curved handle. One little blank carved brass leaf plaque at the roof edge, no text. Warm wooden tones, charcoal brown outlines, muted gold highlights, forest green accents, modest detailing. Paint the whole prop alone, front lower edge horizontal, centered, all feet and handle visible, minimal transparent padding, genuine alpha around the whole prop. No background, ground platform beyond the two posts, ambient haze, smoothed painting, characters, writing, separate items, or UI.
