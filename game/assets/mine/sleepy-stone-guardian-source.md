# 打瞌睡石像像素圖集

《糰糰島》原創礦洞生物素材。用 Codex 內建圖像生成工具製作。外觀以灰紫玄武岩、低彩度青苔和醒來時的琥珀色眼睛為主。

圖集為 64×80 像素，依列切成四張 32×40 透明畫格。腳點是每格左上角往右 16、往下 38 像素。左上和右上是閉眼的睡眠畫格，左下微睜眼，右下睜眼微笑。依原圖寬高共用縮放比例來對齊腳點。原圖與重製步驟在 work/mine-guardian-art。

生成提示如下。

Make one transparent PNG 2 by 2 animation sprite sheet for a original 16-bit cozy Taiwanese island pixel farming game. Subject is the plan's Sleepy Stone Guardian, an adorable small carved weathered basalt guardian sitting in a Taiwanese mine as a friendly obstacle. Single squat rounded granite/basalt body like a chunky stone seal mixed with old trail guardian, tucked tiny hands, wide flat base, slight dusty muted green moss on one shoulder, subtle folded seams and chisel facets. Front three-quarter near top-down tile game camera, looks toward bottom of screen. Dark plum-charcoal outer outline, midtone slate gray purple rock, gray softly luminous lichen accented with muted jade, small amber eye pinpoints when awake only. Keep high local contrast so silhouette reads in a dark cave at native 32 by 40 pixels. STRICT exactly 2 columns and 2 rows with four equally spaced frames, generous clean transparent gutters and no cells touching. Top-left sleeping both eyes closed. Top-right deep sleep with hands exactly same and head idle tiny variation, no floating symbols. Bottom-left newly awake half-open amber eyes, same paws, head and body registration. Bottom-right awake with both big warm amber pupils and a tiny subtle relieved smile. Each frame repeats identical silhouette proportions, same front camera, same brightness except eyes, perfectly registered horizontal foot line and center across its four separate grid cells, no background change. Tiny muted jade on stone only, no floating objects, no bright outline aura. Genuine alpha outside each sprite, no shadow on the ground, no sheet lines or numbers or labels or UI. Each of four figures fully inside its own quadrant. Solid hand-placed visible square pixel clusters, crisp silhouette, no smoothing, dithering, painters strokes or photorealism. Close to a real game sprite intended for frame switching at 32 by 40 pixels per frame.
