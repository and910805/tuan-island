# 打瞌睡石像的三座風鈴

《糰糰島》原創礦洞物件，以 Codex 內建圖像生成工具製作。96×40 透明圖集依序為低音圓形琥珀鈴、中音三角灰綠鈴、高音方形淡紫鈴。每格 32×40，腳點為 (16,38)。用形狀及顏色傳達三個提示，搭配各鈴名稱方便辨認。

原圖及 Godot 轉檔腳本存放於 work/mine-guardian-art。生成提示如下。

Create a single production sprite atlas PNG, three equally spaced cells in one horizontal row, showing three related mine windbell stands for an original cozy Taiwan island 16-bit pixel game. Each sprite is a freestanding bent amber-copper hook fixed to a compact weathered basalt base by the same construction. A suspended tiny bronze bell with a clearly visible round bell bottom and one large, distinct geometric medallion hangs from each. Left stand, honey amber, circular disk. Center stand, muted jade green, upward triangle. Right stand, lavender plum, square. The three visible silhouettes must be visually distinct even in grayscale, with the metal medallion drawn large enough to stay big at native 32×40 px. The stone and copper structure must stay similar across cells with the exact same feet and camera. Top-down three-quarter tile-game perspective from the south, warm edges, dark plunge plum contours, crisp bold visible square pixel clusters, brass light but no glow. Use large intentional color blocks for readability in a dark mine at native resolution. Exactly three complete nonoverlapping isolated sprites with generous clear transparent margin at the sides of all three cells and between them, all on a common horizontal ground baseline. No text, numbers, title or watermarks. No unattached shapes, borders, ground texture, other props, UI or walls. Use genuine transparent alpha around each stand.
